# Self-Evolving Agents

Meta-cognition, meta-learning, and systems that improve themselves

Self-evolving agents represent a leap forward in agentic capability. By incorporating meta-cognition and meta-learning strategies, these agents can reflect on their performance and adapt their strategies over time.
